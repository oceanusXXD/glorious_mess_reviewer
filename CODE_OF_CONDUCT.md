# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes direct, technical, and respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Keep discussion focused on the code, docs, tests, and review workflow.
- Be specific when reporting bugs or proposing changes.
- Assume maintainers need reproducible evidence, not just conclusions.
- Respect that manuscripts, reviewer notes, and safety reports may be sensitive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate, or personal attacks
- Sharing private manuscripts, credentials, or personal data without permission
- Using the project or issue tracker to distribute illegal instructions or abusive content
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after maintainers ask to refocus

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block accounts; or
decline contributions that violate this code of conduct or create avoidable risk.

For sensitive conduct issues, contact maintainers privately through the repository
host's available private channels.
